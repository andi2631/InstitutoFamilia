# Content and copy

This is a Catholic family institute's public site. The text is the product.

- All visible text is Spanish. Match the tone of the existing pages: warm, direct, no marketing slang.
- Never invent or "improve" doctrinal, biographical or institutional text, dates, names, quotes or contact details. If the task needs new copy, take it from what the owner gave you (`Docs/`) or leave a clearly marked placeholder and say so.
- Do not touch `MISION`, the biography timeline in `pages/moreAboutUs.tsx` or the Magisterio quotes except to fix a typo you can prove against the source in `Docs/`.
- External links (articles, organisations, Vatican documents) must be real and checked; do not make up a URL.
- Never commit personal data of attendees or families. Photos in `public/gallery/` are the institute's own; add only photos the owner supplied.
- The contact form sends mail through EmailJS. Do not change the recipient, the service or the template ids without being asked.
- Keep the footer year and any date on the site accurate when you edit near them.
